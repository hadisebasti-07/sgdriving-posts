Singapore traffic lights: amber means stop or go? 🟠

🟠 Amber means STOP, unless you're so close to the stop line that you can't stop safely.
🟢 Flickering green arrow: it's about to change. Not past the stop line? Don't go.
🟠 Amber arrow: get ready to stop behind the line.

Send this to the friend who speeds up at amber 👇
