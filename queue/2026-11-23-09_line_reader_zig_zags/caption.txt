Singapore zig-zag lines: what do they mean? ⚡

⚪ White zig-zags: pedestrian crossing ahead. No stopping, no overtaking, don't wait there.
🟡 One yellow zig-zag: no parking at all times. Quick pick-up or drop-off only.
🔴 Two yellow zig-zags: no stopping at all times.
