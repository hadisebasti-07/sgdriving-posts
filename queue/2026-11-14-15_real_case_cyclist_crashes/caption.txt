Three cyclist crashes in two days in eastern Singapore. Two cyclists died after accidents with tipper trucks, and a third was taken to hospital after an accident with a taxi.

Police are investigating, and our thoughts are with the families of those who died.

What every driver can do:
👀 Shoulder check before you turn
🚲 Cyclist ahead and you're turning left? Slow down and turn BEHIND them. Don't overtake and cut in.
↔️ Passing? Give at least 1.5 m, or wait until it's safe
🐢 Take turns slowly. Cyclists can appear in a second.

If you drive carelessly and someone gets hurt: 9 demerit points and court.

Comment 🚲 if a turning car has ever cut you off 👇
