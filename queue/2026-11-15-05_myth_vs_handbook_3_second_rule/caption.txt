Singapore following distance: is it the 2-second or 3-second rule? 🚗🚗

It's three seconds now (previously two).
When the car ahead passes a fixed point, like a lamp post, count: one-thousand-and-one, one-thousand-and-two, one-thousand-and-three. Reach it before three? You're too close.

🌧️ On a wet road, stopping distance can double, so leave even more room.
