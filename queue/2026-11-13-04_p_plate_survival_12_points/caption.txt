P-plate drivers in Singapore: your demerit limit drops to 12 points from 1 Jan 2027 ⚠️

🟡 Until 31 Dec 2026: probationary licence revoked at 13 demerit points
🔴 From 1 Jan 2027: revoked at 12 points
🟢 Already have points? Probationary drivers get a one-time deduction of up to 1 point

Probation lasts 1 year from the day your licence is issued.
