Singapore bus lane hours: yellow line vs red line 🚌

🟡 Yellow line only (normal bus lane): Mon–Fri, 7.30–9.30am and 5–8pm
🔴 Yellow + red line (full-day bus lane): Mon–Sat, 7.30am–11pm
🟢 Sundays and public holidays: neither is in operation
↩️ Broken yellow line: you may cross it to turn into a side road

Save this before your Saturday drive 📌

Source: Traffic Police, Basic Theory of Driving (2 Jan 2026) para 54; LTA OneMotoring. Paraphrased. Not affiliated with TP or LTA.
