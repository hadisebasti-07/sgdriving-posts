A self-driving shuttle in Punggol was filmed passing just metres from a schoolgirl crossing the road 😳

Now imagine YOU were the driver. Green light, turning left, a child steps out. GO or WAIT? 👇

If a driver did this:
⚠️ Careless driving endangering life: 6 demerit points, S$200
🚑 If someone is hurt: 9 points and court, up to 1 year's jail
🔰 On P plates: from 1 Jan 2027, 12 points = licence revoked

Green means go only when it's safe. When you turn, look out for people crossing.
