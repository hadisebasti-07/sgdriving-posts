Singapore yellow box junction: can you enter on a green light? 🟨

If your exit is jammed, don't go in. Entering a yellow box and blocking it is an offence, even when the light is green.

You MAY wait in the box when:
1️⃣ Turning, without blocking other traffic
2️⃣ Waiting to turn right for oncoming traffic
3️⃣ Waiting to turn while pedestrians cross

Send this to the friend who always blocks the junction 👇
