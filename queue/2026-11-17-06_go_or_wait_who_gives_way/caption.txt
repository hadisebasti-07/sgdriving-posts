Singapore uncontrolled junction: no lights, no signs. Who gives way? 🚦❌

➡️ Going straight: give way to traffic going straight from your right.
⬅️ Turning left: give way to traffic going straight from your right.
↪️ Turning right: give way to traffic going straight from ALL directions, plus right-turners from your right and left-turners from ahead.
Not sure? Look right, left, then right again.

Send this to the friend who never gives way 👇

Source: Traffic Police, Basic Theory of Driving (2 Jan 2026) paras 81–83. Paraphrased. Not affiliated with TP or LTA.
