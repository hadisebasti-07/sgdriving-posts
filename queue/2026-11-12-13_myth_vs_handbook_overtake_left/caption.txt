Overtaking on the left in Singapore: always wrong? Not always 👀

The handbook says always overtake on the right, except:
1️⃣ The driver in front has signalled to turn right
2️⃣ You want to turn left at a junction
3️⃣ Traffic is moving slowly in queues, and vehicles in the lanes on your right are slower than you
4️⃣ One-way streets (but not dual carriageways)

If in doubt, don't overtake.
