Singapore driving: can you turn right on a green light? 🚦

Plain green ≠ green arrow.
✅ Plain green: you can turn right, but give way to oncoming traffic first.
✅ Green arrow: go, after checking oncoming traffic is clear.
🛑 Red right-turn arrow: wait. Even if the road is empty.
