Your mirror says it's clear. It isn't. 🏍️

Changing lanes in Singapore? The handbook order:
1️⃣ Check your mirrors
2️⃣ Signal, at least 3 seconds ahead
3️⃣ Turn your head and check the blind spot
4️⃣ Clear? Adjust your speed and steer gently across
5️⃣ Cancel your signal

Changing lanes recklessly is an offence.
