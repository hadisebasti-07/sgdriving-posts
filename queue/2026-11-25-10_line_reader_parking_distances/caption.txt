Singapore parking rules: how far from a junction, bus stop or fire hydrant? 📏

Don't park within:
🚧 6 m of a junction
🚌 9 m of a bus stop (from the yellow box or bus bay)
🧯 3 m either side of a fire hydrant
Also never in a bus lane, on a bend, or facing oncoming traffic.

Save this before you park 📌

Source: Traffic Police, Basic Theory of Driving (2 Jan 2026) para 97. Paraphrased. Not affiliated with TP or LTA.
